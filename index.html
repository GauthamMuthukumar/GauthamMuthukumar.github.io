About Me

I'm a Principal Product Manager with 15+ years of experience building AI, ML and platform products at some of the world's leading technology companies — Intuit, Microsoft, LinkedIn and Amazon.

My focus is on the intersection of AI infrastructure and end-user value: I've built agentic platforms used by millions of small businesses, distilled large language models at scale, and designed ML pipelines that process hundreds of millions of documents a year.
